Case Study

Meesho: E-commerce Experience

Redesigning the checkout and discovery flow to reduce friction and improve conversion rates for millions of users across India.

Role

Lead UX Designer

Duration

3 Months

Tools

Figma, Miro, Maze

Platform

iOS & Android

The Challenge

Despite a massive user base, the existing platform experienced a significant drop-off rate during the final stages of the checkout process. The cognitive load was high due to cluttered information architecture, unclear pricing breakdowns, and a disjointed address selection flow. The primary objective was to streamline this journey without alienating the existing user demographic accustomed to the legacy interface.

Research & Insights

group_remove

68% Drop-off

Analytics revealed that a majority of users abandoned their carts at the payment method selection stage due to overwhelming options and hidden fees.

psychology

Cognitive Overload

User testing confirmed that the visual hierarchy was competing rather than guiding. Essential actions like "Apply Coupon" were visually indistinguishable from secondary information.

visibility View Full Report

Priya, 28

Target Persona

"I just want to see the final price quickly before I decide to pay. Sometimes it feels like there are too many steps."

Value Seeker Mobile Primary Time-sensitive

The Solution

We introduced a collapsible accordion pattern for checkout steps, keeping the context visible while reducing vertical scrolling. The visual hierarchy was strictly enforced using typography and color.
